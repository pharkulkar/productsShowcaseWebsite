About

Built by a team.
Used by real teams.

We're a small team of developers building focused, no-bloat software for people who do actual work — crypto traders, restaurateurs, growth marketers, and anyone stuck in too many meetings. No fluff, no bloat — just tools that solve real problems.

The story

Every tool here started as a personal frustration. We needed real-time BTC context without opening five tabs. We wanted to pull Google Business data without paying for enterprise APIs. We sat through enough meetings where we knew exactly nothing about the person we were talking to. We built solutions for ourselves, then polished them until other people could use them too.

We're a team of two developers who contribute to every product — from design and architecture to shipping and support. No investors, no roadmap decks, no quarterly OKRs. Just shipping when something is ready, fixing what breaks, and listening to users who care enough to send feedback.

The stack varies by product — Chrome extensions, desktop apps, web platforms — but the philosophy is consistent: do one thing well and stay out of the user's way.

The products

  • ◈ Whisper AI Real-time meeting assistant with invisible overlay
  • ◈ BTC Lens Chrome extension for live Bitcoin news and price data
  • ◈ GMB Extractor Google Business data extraction for growth teams
  • ◈ TableFlow Operations platform built for restaurant teams

Values

Ship fast, fix faster

A working product in users' hands beats a perfect spec in a doc. We ship early, watch what breaks, and fix it within hours — not sprints.

No bloat

Every feature earns its place. If it doesn't solve a real problem someone actually has, it doesn't ship. Small, focused tools age well.

Privacy first

No ad networks, no selling user data, no surveillance. What you do with these tools stays with you. That's the default, not an upsell.
