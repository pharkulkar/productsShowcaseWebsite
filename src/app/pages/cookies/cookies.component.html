Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners.

This site uses browser local storage (a similar technology) rather than traditional cookies to store your consent preference. The effect is identical: a small piece of data is stored in your browser.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential (always active)

  • cookie_consent — stores your accept/decline decision for this notice. Expires when cleared or reset. Required to avoid re-showing the banner on every visit.
  • Session management — short-lived session identifiers used by the server to serve pages correctly. Not tied to any personal profile.

Analytics (optional)

We may introduce privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., aggregate page-view counts with no personally identifiable information) only if you opt in via the consent banner. No analytics are active by default.

Advertising / Tracking

We use none. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, and no fingerprinting scripts on this site.

3. How to Control Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All

Note: deleting essential storage may cause the cookie banner to reappear on your next visit.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We currently use no third-party cookie-setting scripts. If this changes (for example, if we embed a video or payment widget), this policy will be updated before deployment.

5. Updates to This Policy

If we add new cookies or tracking technologies, we will update this page and, where required by law, request fresh consent. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
