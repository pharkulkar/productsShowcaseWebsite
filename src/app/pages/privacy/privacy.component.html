1. Introduction & Who We Are

My Products is operated by a small team of developers based in Bengaluru, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when using our website (myproducts.dev) and any of our associated products.

By using our site or products, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate this site:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, subject, and message content when you choose to send us a message.
  • Cookie consent preference: a single local storage flag recording whether you accepted or declined our essential cookie notice.
  • Server access logs: IP address, browser type, and pages visited — retained briefly for security and operational purposes.

We do not run persistent analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or third-party session recording tools by default.

3. How We Use Your Data

Contact form submissions are used exclusively to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists, share your details with third parties, or use your information for advertising purposes.

Server access logs are used solely for diagnosing technical issues and identifying security threats.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.

The only third-party service that may process your data is our website hosting provider, which processes server traffic in order to serve pages to you. This provider is contractually bound to data protection standards consistent with applicable law.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This site uses first-party session cookies only. We do not set advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts. For a full breakdown, see our Cookie Policy.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR (if you are in the European Economic Area) and CCPA (if you are in California), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion: request that we delete your personal data.
  • Right to portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: object to any processing of your data.
  • Right to correction: request correction of inaccurate information.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@myproducts.dev. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of 12 months from the date of receipt, then permanently deleted. Server access logs are retained for no more than 90 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site and products are not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us immediately at privacy@myproducts.dev and we will delete it promptly.
